Title: Army Fitness Test Overview
Author: United States Army
Topic: AFT Overview
Source: U.S. Army AFT Website

The Army Fitness Test became the Army's physical fitness test of record on June 1, 2025.

The AFT contains five events:

1. Three-Repetition Maximum Deadlift
2. Hand-Release Push-Up
3. Sprint-Drag-Carry
4. Plank
5. Two-Mile Run

The general AFT standard is based on age and sex.

Soldiers using the general standard must earn at least 60 points in each event and at least 300 total points.
