Title: Army Fitness Test Events
Author: United States Army
Topic: AFT Events
Source: U.S. Army AFT Website

The Army Fitness Test contains five events.

The Three-Repetition Maximum Deadlift measures muscular strength.

The Hand-Release Push-Up measures upper-body muscular endurance.

The Sprint-Drag-Carry measures muscular strength, muscular endurance, anaerobic power, and anaerobic endurance.

The Plank measures muscular endurance and balance.

The Two-Mile Run measures aerobic endurance.
